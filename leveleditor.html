How to build the map

  1. Choose PATH.
  2. Click cells that the robot can use.
  3. Choose ROBOT and click the starting cell.
  4. Choose GOAL and click the goal cell.
  5. For Repetition, choose a building tool and click the target area.
  6. Choose the starting direction.
  7. Select the blocks students can use.
  8. Set the maximum number of blocks students can use.
  9. Click SAVE LEVEL.
